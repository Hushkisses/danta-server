# DEV-055 — Army food / supply-state baseline

Status: COMPLETE — Windows automated + Paper EconomyTick verification passed

## Goal
Design v0.3 defines food as fuel for military action rather than a normal standing-army upkeep resource. Normal stationing should consume almost nothing; movement, enemy advance, combat, siege and landing consume much more. Exact army/troop food formulas remain explicitly unfinalized in v0.3, and selectable expedition stockpiles belong to DEV-056.

## Minimal implementation
- ArmySupplyService consumes FOOD on each 30-minute EconomyTick.
- Provisional per-1000-troop rates:
  - STATIONED: 1 food/tick
  - MOVING: 20 food/tick
  - IN_BATTLE: 40 food/tick
- Troops are rounded up by 1000 for this temporary baseline.
- Consumption never makes national FOOD negative.
- A shortage is reported by the service and EconomyTick log.
- EconomyTick production is settled first, then army consumption.
- These numeric rates are explicitly provisional and must be replaced during balancing; they are not claimed as final v0.3 numbers.

## Scope boundary
DEV-055 does not yet implement selectable army carried-food amounts, because DEV-056 owns light/standard/heavy expedition supply. It also does not yet apply shortage penalties to movement/combat; later combat/supply tickets consume the shortage state.

## Automated tests
Dev055ArmySupplyTest verifies:
- stationed consumption is tiny relative to movement/combat;
- 1/20/40 provisional rate distinction;
- shortage cannot drive FOOD below zero and is reported.

## Windows verification
1. Stop server, pull main, run quick-deploy. Automated tests must pass.
2. Start Paper and confirm normal boot/snapshot recovery.
3. Set red FOOD to a known amount and create/use a 1000-troop stationed army.
4. Cross one EconomyTick and verify the EconomyTick log reports army food consumption and national FOOD changes by the expected amount after production.
5. Repeat with a moving army to verify substantially higher consumption.

Do not mark COMPLETE until automated tests and Paper EconomyTick integration pass.

## Verification result
Windows Paper verification passed on 2026-09-15. A 1000-troop stationed army consumed 1 FOOD/tick; the same army in MOVING state consumed 20 FOOD/tick. EconomyTick production and subsequent consumption matched expected totals.
