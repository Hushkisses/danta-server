# DEV-083 — ResearchDefinition data loader

Status: IMPLEMENTED — Windows automated build/test verification pending.

## Goal
Introduce the Paper-independent, data-driven definition layer for research content. Nation completion state, queues, scheduling, slot count, activation/deactivation and doctrine selection are intentionally deferred to DEV-084+.

## Source alignment
- Four fields are represented: MILITARY / INDUSTRY / ADMINISTRATION / MAGIC.
- Five tiers are represented: TIER_1 through TIER_5.
- Duration, GOLD cost, strategic-resource costs, prerequisites, doctrine key and required-major-point type are content data rather than hard-coded balance constants.
- Exact full research tree, prerequisite graph and doctrine values remain unresolved in design v0.3 and are not invented here.

## Validation
The YAML loader rejects malformed definitions, duplicate IDs, unknown prerequisite IDs, self-dependencies and prerequisite cycles. Tier 4 is treated as the doctrine stage and therefore requires a doctrineKey.

## Intentionally not implemented
- Nation ResearchState / completion persistence
- Runtime research queue/scheduler
- Research cancellation/refund
- Research slot 1→2
- Doctrine slot 2→3
- Major-point loss deactivation
- Sample/full research tree

Those belong to DEV-084 through DEV-088.
