# DEV-091 — Support participation and direct-alliance automatic war entry

Status: IMPLEMENTED — Windows automated/Paper verification pending.

## Product rules from design v0.3
- A direct alliance partner automatically enters war when its ally enters war.
- Automatic participation does not chain through allies-of-allies.
- Automatic war-state participation does not force actual army deployment; army support remains a player decision.
- Support participation is an explicit nation-level entry into one specific war. Individual mercenary participation is not supported.

## Implemented
- War aggregate with explicit attacker/defender nation sets and stable UUID.
- WarService declaration path sets belligerent relations to WAR and adds direct alliance partners once, from the declaration-time relationship graph.
- No recursive alliance propagation.
- Explicit supportJoin for a nation to attacker or defender side.
- Joining against one's own alliance is rejected.
- Korean development commands: diplomacy war / support / wars.
- Core JUnit coverage for direct-only automatic participation, explicit support participation, and alliance conflict rejection.

## Deliberately deferred
- Passage/supply rights are DEV-092.
- Vassal automatic defense and military-diplomacy restrictions are DEV-095/096.
- War persistence is not introduced in DEV-091 because the execution plan does not yet define war lifecycle/end-state persistence semantics; relationship state itself remains Snapshot-persisted from DEV-090. Before war state becomes season-authoritative, lifecycle + persistence must be implemented together rather than guessing an incomplete format.
