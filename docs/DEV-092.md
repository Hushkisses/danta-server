# DEV-092 — Passage / supply rights

Status: IMPLEMENTED — Windows verification pending.

## Product interpretation
Design v0.3 states that FRIENDLY grants passage, trade and material support, while ALLIANCE includes FRIENDLY benefits plus automatic direct-alliance war participation. Therefore DEV-092 derives both passage and supply rights for FRIENDLY and ALLIANCE. NEUTRAL and WAR do not grant either right. Rights are modeled separately even where current relationship tiers grant the same pair, so later treaties/rules can diverge without rewriting callers.

## Implemented
- AccessRight.PASSAGE and AccessRight.SUPPLY.
- DiplomaticAccessService derives rights from DEV-090 authoritative bilateral relationship state; no duplicate persisted permission state.
- Own territory permits both; neutral/unowned point entry remains distinct from foreign permission (unowned points can be entered, but cannot serve as a foreign supply-right point).
- Point-level canEnterPoint / canUsePointForSupply policy hooks for later movement/supply integration.
- Korean development command: /danta diplomacy access <이용국> <영토국>.
- JUnit coverage for neutral/war denial, friendly/alliance grant, own-territory access.

## Integration boundary
DEV-092 establishes the reusable permission policy. Existing DEV-031 movement and DEV-054 own-territory supply connectivity are not silently rewritten into a broader allied logistics model in this ticket because route-through-allied-territory logistics semantics and hostile-entry/occupation interaction require their own integration rules. Callers can now consume the policy explicitly.
